# Final Evidence: Time-Distributed Partitioned Convolution

Date: 2026-07-23

## Implementation

`PartitionedConvolver` keeps the public 1024-frame uniform partition layout.
The older tail passes are accumulated as preallocated, partition-major quanta
while the current partition fills. A Bresenham-style frame scheduler and direct
channel/partition/history cursors make the quantum order independent of callback
chunking without a per-frame division or modulo. The newest history-slot pass
and inverse FFT remain at the boundary; the forward FFT commits the completed
input block. No thread, lock, hot-path allocation, I/O, logging, or public API
change was introduced.

The persistent accumulator is cleared after each inverse FFT. `reset` clears
the accumulator, scheduler, history, overlap, and input state together.

## Same-Process Regression

`convolver-spread-abba-direct-cursor-final.json` alternates baseline/candidate
measurements in one pinned process, retains all nine trial samples, and records
paired ratios. Across 10 IR-size/channel cases, the largest paired median
regression is `+5.203%`, below the 10% gate. The largest independent median in
that report is `+5.844%`. The maximum output delta is the value recorded above.

The earlier repeat reports remain useful as variance evidence:
`convolver-spread-abba-direct-cursor.json` had a largest paired median of
`+2.826%`, and `convolver-spread-abba-direct-cursor-repeat.json` had `+7.042%`.
